# Changes — 1.27.0

Current-ability selection now applies across the objective system. Pushups were an example of a wider mismatch; they are no longer the only activity with a measured route to suitable work.

Open a request and choose **Check current ability**. Perform a sample, name the specific subject and comparison source/setup, and record which criteria were met. A capable learner can start with independent or constrained work immediately. Saving a sample grants no XP, stats or skills. Registration also offers a profession sample, retained through reloads.

Daily results can opt into adapting future work. Two comparable independent checks on different days move the suggestion up one tier; difficulty, source version, subject and activity remain distinct. Mental/career checks require unfamiliar work. Raw correct/attempted counts override a conflicting self-rating. A harder unsuccessful check resets the comparison frontier instead of repeatedly promoting from old easy successes. Samples older than 90 days do not drive automatic placement. Daily suggestions stop at C; mastery calls for harder source material or a prepared project.

Language, writing, listening, message drafting, contextual Scripture study, judgment, coordination and mobility retain activity-specific work. All twenty career categories have four-part output rubrics, and all twenty-five vocations keep their own focus. Career milestone exams now compare profession work even when the category's game stat is DEX, STR or CON. They no longer require an unrelated fitness proxy in the interface.

Training adapts pressing, legs, pulling, hinges and core independently. Comparable top-range loaded sessions suggest a 2.5% increase for that movement; zero-reserve loaded work suggests a 5% reduction. Bodyweight setups progress separately, core A/B patterns remain distinct, and self-resistance rows do not fabricate measurable overload. Initial chair/pullup evidence affects its relevant setup, with a secure bar required for home pullups. Deliberate manual/recovery plans can turn automatic progression off. Walking requests honor the chosen five-minute-step target rather than silently rounding to a different request.

Existing accepted contracts and old workout recipes remain readable and fixed. Result evidence credits the class/profession/vocation that originally accepted the work. Ability samples appear automatically in Journey and travel in character backups and chunked primary history. RPG stat names, stat milestones, exam gates, guild thresholds, purchased skills and midnight HP/MP recovery remain.
