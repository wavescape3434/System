# Version 1.24.0

Fighter techniques now distinguish swords, spears, axes and unarmed combat. The Mage tree adds Pyromancy, Frostcraft and Stormcalling, each with three ranks and one-slot upgrades. Inspect the target’s elemental matchup in battle and open **Technique details** for the current actor’s commands.

- Swords reward guarding; spears intercept charged attacks and penetrate defense; axes wear down armor; unarmed techniques use DEX power where stronger and cost less MP.
- Ice reduces enemy attack damage for two rounds, including bosses. Storm spells arc to other enemies. Elemental weaknesses/resistances affect new elemental spells and fire burns; neutral spells remain neutral.
- DEX-led attackers and WIS-led healers can use their focused stat as a stronger combat source. Base stats stay unchanged.
- Twelve Prestige/Ascendant classes gain six signature-effect families: protection after a strike, protection with restoration, aether burns, wounded-target executions, MP echoes and spreading burns. Equipped learned focus variants retain those effects after class changes.
- Existing skill ownership, purchase records, prerequisites and four-slot limits remain. New effects are copied into new fights; older active battles retain their existing rules.

The [build report](builds-1.24/README.md) compares 5,424 synthetic fights across all 51 classes, loadouts, focused stats and party setups. The separate [ninety-day economy report](economy-1.24/README.md) follows 32 earning/purchasing accounts for 2,880 account-days, including calendar and career work.

Gold surplus remains; neither report establishes final balance. Companion-led builds, earned progress, both gold sources, unrestricted exploration and automatic local-midnight recovery are retained. Google setup remains deferred; physical Android runtime testing remains open.
