# A more useful fantasy attribute set — proposal only

The current STR / DEX / CON / INT / WIS / CHA names and all progress remain unchanged in 1.26. The owner rejected a plain-language replacement and requested a more fantasy-oriented design, drawing from anime and RPG systems.

My recommendation is **seven attributes: Might, Agility, Vitality, Intellect, Perception, Resolve, Presence**.

The design borrows the separation of physical abilities seen in *DanMachi*, perception/awareness as an independent attribute seen in *Solo Leveling*, and the distinction between scholarly knowledge and resolve/spirit common in RPG character systems. These are design inspirations; the app is not copying those games' formulas or presenting their fictional attribute systems as research.

| Attribute | Improvement goal | Evidence to retain | Adventure identity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Might** | Become stronger | Comparable pushing, pulling, squat/hinge loads, ranges and clean repetitions | Force, heavy weapons, lifting and breaking obstacles |
| **Agility** | Move accurately and control a skill | Specific footwork, hand coordination, balance and accuracy tests | Precision, evasive techniques and controlled movement |
| **Vitality** | Sustain useful effort | Same-route times, sustainable activity and training tolerance | Endurance and durable defensive roles |
| **Intellect** | Learn and reason within real subjects | Unfamiliar reasoning problems, retrieval and checked work samples | Research, construction, magical technique and analysis |
| **Perception** | Notice useful clues and judge uncertainty | Observations, recorded predictions, calibration and later feedback | Scouting, investigation, finding weaknesses and avoiding traps |
| **Resolve** | Follow through and regulate effort | A longer record of chosen commitments, recovery-aware consistency and adaptation after setbacks | Concentration, maintaining an action, resisting disruption and rallying |
| **Presence** | Express ideas and understand others | Recorded explanations, accurate summaries, audience adaptation and feedback | Leadership, negotiation, performance and support |

Why seven: the current WIS combines intuition, judgment, reflection and faith. Perception can focus on noticing and interpreting evidence, while Resolve gives long-term self-direction a separate role. Christian study, prayer and service remain explicit activities and class goals; faith is not a numerical worth score.

**Resolve needs a new design, not an instant quiz.** I would start it unassessed at the neutral game value, accumulate useful evidence over weeks, and let chosen commitments and adaptation count without subtracting points for missed days. It must not become a second punishment-based streak counter. Perception and Resolve need clear progression/reward/combat rules before implementation.

A migration would preserve every earned value and class/skill requirement. Most current mappings have an obvious destination: STR→Might, DEX→Agility, CON→Vitality, INT→Intellect, CHA→Presence. WIS→Perception is only partial; its earned history and faith-related class identity need an explicit migration. Existing WIS points should not be silently removed or split, and Resolve should not be fabricated from old unrelated receipts.

This proposal is ready for discussion; it is **not** applied to player accounts.
