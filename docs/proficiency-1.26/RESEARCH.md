# Objective efficacy and assessment review — 1.26

Reviewed 28 October 2026 (America/Denver).

The problem is a mismatch between work and demonstrated ability. A guild rank, a class level, and a person's ability in a particular exercise or subject are three different things. In 1.25 a floor-pushup assessment could only suggest E session volume, while the E home recipe still used incline pushups. The board also selected the first available variant, typically the easiest, and career/class daily work often had only one routine difficulty.

## Physical work

- **Pushing:** floor maxima 0 / 1–7 / 8–29 / 30+ suggest wall / incline / floor / harder floor practice. The thresholds select a starting setup, not a published percentile or a universal strength diagnosis. A 35-floor-pushup result suggests a harder setup immediately, even while the guild card remains F.
- **Workload:** workout sets still follow F–C session volume. The pushing setup follows its own ability measure, independently of legs, pulling, hips and core. A strong pushing result alone does not assign an advanced full-body program.
- **Effort:** calibrated home movements target 6–12 controlled reps with about 2–3 clean reps left. A low feet elevation or slow lowering phase can make pushing harder; if the minimum is not controlled, reduce difficulty. An easy set is a warm-up, not the whole prescribed stimulus.
- **Legs, hips, core:** independent home setup selectors allow chair → squat → supported split squat → slow split squat; bridge → pause → staggered stance → single leg; and short → standard → longer/paused → slower reach. Use a practice set to choose capacity. These are options, not mechanically equivalent tests.
- **Weights/gym:** keep familiar compounds, explicit external loads and effort logs. A home floor-pushup result is not used as a machine-press load estimate. Increase measured load only after comparable successful work, not from an RPG stat.
- **Pulling:** self-resisted rowing remains a fallback with poorly quantified load. For measurable pulling progression use the weights/backpack or gym route. The app does not pretend more self-resisted repetitions prove stronger pulling.
- **Aerobic work:** registration asks for one-mile minutes and seconds, separately labeled walk or run/run-walk. The same distance is 1.609 km in metric. A recorded mile starts the walking suggestion at 15 minutes instead of automatically five. Duration and running readiness still need separate judgment. Older six-minute-walk results remain valid and available as an alternative protocol.
- **Coordination/mobility:** select the skill, setup, accuracy and criterion separately; practice count alone does not establish general dexterity. Existing graded drill variants remain available.

## Knowledge, profession and vocation work

Each selected profession and vocation has F–C daily variants. A new learner reproduces a small part after a worked example; foundation work uses familiar independent examples; independent work uses unfamiliar standard cases; challenging work adds constraints, comparison and verification. The maintained twenty career guides supply category-specific assignments. A vocation retains its own practice focus.

Ability settings hold a **specific subject**, **chosen starting proficiency** and **success criterion/source** independently for class focus, profession, vocation and universal activity family. Registration can set a profession subject and initial level. An expert in a field can choose challenging work immediately; a beginner can choose F. Neither owns extra skills, stat gains or credentials by doing so.

Two separate-day checked results on the same topic and chosen rank can suggest a review: both at least 85% → try a harder unfamiliar sample; both below 60% → revisit a prerequisite. Reviews do not automatically change the plan or award a level. The app cannot independently verify that a marking guide, source version or problem set was comparable. Minutes and topic names are not proof of mastery.

Christian Scripture practice keeps contextual interpretation, checked supporting text and application. Judgment practice can use recorded predictions and later outcomes. Spoken practice checks an actual produced explanation and revision. None of these scores represents faith, morality or spiritual worth.

## Initial mental checks

**INT:** five original problems drawn from ten reasoning families: deduction, conditional requirements, constrained order, specified transformations, set overlap, independent events, truth labels, rate reasoning, necessary versus sufficient evidence, and falsification. Arithmetic is subordinate to the reasoning task.

**WIS:** ten families cover base rates, a backward-growth puzzle, sample reliability, prediction calibration, sunk costs, causal uncertainty, experienced intuition with feedback, robust summaries, nondiagnostic clues and evidence updates. This includes intuitive errors and a puzzle without treating every riddle as wisdom.

**CHA:** five performed exercises: explain to a beginner, compress an explanation, adapt to two audiences, summarize an unfamiliar recording, and teach a short process. Each has four observable criteria and an anchored 0–3 self-review. Optional microphone recording is explicitly initiated, locally replayable, bounded to the exercise plus thirty seconds, and discarded on leaving that exercise. Permission denial leaves aloud practice usable. **The app does not automatically judge voice quality, personality or communication mastery.**

All questions/exercises remain hidden until Start. Newly started forms use v3; v2 scoring and records stay supported. A changed assessment version establishes a fresh milestone baseline instead of comparing unlike scores. Faster completion alone cannot pass a v3 improvement exam. Already earned levels and rewards remain. Work samples remain an alternate route when a brief check reaches its ceiling.

## Calibration and remaining work

The six original stat names stay unchanged at the owner's direction. Scores are uncapped RPG indices. Published grip/chair/walk references remain where applicable; a **16-minute walk / 10-minute run mile anchor** is explicitly provisional and is not claimed to equal the average adult. Missing assessments stay neutral at 10. A new published norm, reliability study or controlled user trial is required before claiming more.

Weekly/monthly calendars, manual acceptance, advancement exams, rewards and long-term guild thresholds remain. The broader prepared achievements are not newly certified sport standards or professional qualifications. Accepted prescriptions retain their exact work/reward snapshots. New grade variants share a completion family, preventing same-period reward claims at several difficulties.

Remaining priorities: an actual short subject-placement sampler with trusted discipline-specific banks; externally reviewed professional rubrics; personal aerobic-volume history; substitutions and independent partial-work logs; assessment form equivalence, confidence/error analysis and ceilings; and observed human improvement/adherence. This pass fixes selection and adds calibration tools; it does not claim a complete individualized clinical or professional curriculum.
