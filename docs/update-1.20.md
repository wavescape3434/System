# Version 1.20.0 — Fieldwork for every profession category

All **twenty profession categories** now have an initial learned Fieldwork tool. This release adds the final six categories. Buy a listed root skill or opening primer, then open its tool from the skill panel, profession record or Adventures → Fieldwork. Ownership remains after changing professions.

| Category | Tool | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arts, Design & Media | Cartographer’s Folio | Inspect a visual three-area route, stage marks, exploration access, lifetime visits and landmarks retained in the journal. Open an accessible area. |
| Sales & Marketing | Broker’s Exchange | Filter delivery offers by unlocked town and material, compare actual gold per required item, acceptance and shortages, then visit the selected guild board. |
| Administration | Archivist’s Register | Reconcile actual commission receipts over seven/thirty calendar days or all retained past receipts; show town subtotals and export the selected period. |
| Hospitality & Food | Wayfarer’s Concierge | Compare live store stocks, prices, smith tiers, recipe counts and restocking commissions. Visit an unlocked town’s shops. |
| Manufacturing & Quality | Forge Work Order | Combine multiple current-town recipes into one gold/material bill, show shortages and accessible sources, then open the blacksmith. |
| General Practice | Adventurer’s Departure Check | Check current route progress, party availability, fallen/low-HP members, MP shortages, equipped skills and accepted local commissions. |

The folio supports phone and desktop layouts and preserves legacy area access. Its stage marks summarize progress; the normal map still shows a single current-stage box.

Offer comparisons are alternatives: inventory consumed by one delivery cannot also pay another. Receipt totals are recorded commission income, separate from spending, battle rewards, login rewards and real-world request rewards. Repeated commissions retain their individual receipt IDs and recorded amounts.

The crafting basket is temporary view state. It neither crafts nor spends and is not part of saved character plans. Existing shop actions remain authoritative. Explicit town visits end the expedition and preserve HP/MP; closing Fieldwork retains the expedition. Travel is not a rest. Full recovery still occurs at local midnight.

No character-schema migration, new dependencies or changes to real-world XP/rank thresholds. Android **1.20.0 / code 22** keeps the existing package and signing certificate.

P07’s initial category coverage is complete; later-node utilities, deliverable review and skill mastery remain open. P08 is next: simulate income, supplies and equipment spending before tuning the economy. Physical Android checks remain open; Google setup stays deferred.
