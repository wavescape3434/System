# Version 1.10 — Pixel sprites and portraits

All 24 appearances now use a stronger pixel-art treatment, with clearer stepped contours, compact shading, slender proportions, and the existing outfit variety. Moonsilk Dancer, Velvet Enchantress, Crimson Corsair, and the rest of the roster keep their saved IDs. Existing selections continue to work after the update.

Settings → Appearance and the registration/party appearance chooser offer **Full sprites** and **Portraits** preview modes. The selected mode only changes the preview. Use still commits the chosen appearance.

The battle turn queue now shows close-up human portraits, with individual face framing for each of the 24 designs. Full-body sprites remain on the battlefield. The seven companions share the updated rendering, and registration/Settings profile summaries use the same portraits.

The phone formation gives the third party member enough room to stay inside the battlefield. Browser checks measure the complete sprite bounds as well as the fighter controls. The engraved Guild Card remains as before.

The revised atlas is bundled under a new filename to refresh artwork on an upgraded installation. Appearance IDs, character storage, class choices, combat abilities, inventory, and progression retain their existing rules. These remain standing battle poses with the existing combat effects.
