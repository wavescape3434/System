# Version 1.21.0 — supplies and economy evidence

Adventures now has a **Supplies** dialog on the map and during expeditions. Choose a living party member and use one carried potion or ether. The preview shows the actual recovery: up to 50 HP or 20 MP, capped by what is missing. Full and fallen targets cannot consume an item. Recovery and inventory changes save together. Closing the dialog keeps the expedition; the automatic full recovery remains local midnight.

General stores accept **whole purchase quantities from 1 to 99**. The card shows the batch price and any gold/stock shortage. A purchase succeeds for the entire batch or leaves gold, inventory and town stock unchanged. Single-item purchases retain their existing prices. Equipment purchases remain one unique item.

Delivery cards show the exact material cost and remaining inventory before turn-in. If it would remove material coverage for an unowned smith recipe, expand **Smith recipes affected** to review the alternatives. The app does not reserve materials or block a deliberate delivery.

Every town gains a **CHA seal accessory**, using the existing smith price/material/modifier scale. There are now **36 town recipes**. Equipping a seal changes combat CHA and can strengthen support skills; base CHA and real-world XP do not change. Battles also refuse full/fallen-target potion or ether use without spending stock or advancing the turn.

The [economy report](economy-1.21/README.md) compares **80 baseline and 80 updated thirty-day accounts**, with seven-day checkpoints, three/five-attempt budgets, varied Basic classes, material priorities, exploration seeds and higher-stat sensitivity cases. It includes two figures, source hashes, 4,800 daily observations and validated sample accounts.

The owner’s direction is to support roughly **3–5 encounters per day**, retain both gold sources and tune spending/material demand. No encounter cap is added. This pass keeps current prices, reward rates, login rewards and guild-rank thresholds. The report identifies gold surplus, companion carry, finite campaign breadth and high-stat challenge as remaining work; it does not claim final economy balance.

Android **1.21.0 / code 23** retains the package and signing certificate. No character-schema migration or new application dependency. Google setup stays deferred; physical Android checks remain open.
