# The Adventurers Guild 1.5.0

## Assessment

- Training includes **Gym equipment**, which satisfies weighted and pullup-bar availability. Bodyweight remains usable without a gym.
- Assessment has a metric/imperial selector. Height uses cm or separate feet and inches; stored measurements remain metric. Result and history displays follow the selected units.
- Pushups and strict pullups explicitly ask for maximum consecutive repetitions. Walking and timed checks specify their duration and unit.
- The bottom button advances Body → INT → WIS → CHA → Results. During a mental check it advances the questions first.
- Questions appear only after Start. INT, WIS, and CHA each have 24 five-question scenario sets: 120 distinct prompts per attribute before case patterns recur. Started forms, completed forms, and class exam forms are remembered so the next check uses another form. Later sets can reuse patterns; this is not an unlimited unique exam bank.
- No test includes Bible verses. Christian study, prayer, and service remain real-world objectives.

The published physical references and provisional average-10 anchors remain documented in the [assessment audit](../metrics-audit.md). The original mental checks have no published population norms or validated equivalence between forms. Their scores and timing support the game's progression rules; they do not establish intelligence, wisdom, personality, or clinical change.

## Five-level improvement exams

**Class Paths → Examinations** lists the next class and profession milestones. Both training XP and relevant class/profession work must qualify before an exam becomes available. Level 5, 10, 15, and each later fifth level waits for a passed exam; XP remains banked. Passing awards the milestone and its focused stat points, then releases other already-earned levels until the next exam. Player level remains independent.

Physical paths use their focus attribute and offer alternate protocols. There are four pushup variations, chair stands, grip, an eight-repetition bench load, strict pullups, balance, target taps, controlled line steps, six- and twelve-minute walks, and a fixed-route walking time. Equipment, setup, variation, time limit, and technique must stay consistent for a comparison. The earliest matching assessment supplies the initial baseline; subsequent milestones compare with the last passed result for the same path and protocol. Recent routine checks do not silently replace the exam baseline.

- Increasing metrics need at least **5% improvement and one additional recorded unit**. Count-based protocols require whole counts, apart from the mean of two chair-stand trials.
- A lower-is-better walking time needs at least a **5% reduction**.
- Balance ends at 45 seconds; use an alternate protocol after reaching its ceiling.
- A new alternate protocol records a baseline first. Improvement must be recorded on a later local calendar day, including when the initial baseline came from registration.

Mental paths use their INT/WIS/CHA focus. A fresh form must score at least **4/5**, with improved accuracy over the previous baseline/pass, or maintained accuracy with at least **5% less recorded time**. A repeatable ten-minute work sample is the alternative: record correct outcomes with a written marking guide, establish its baseline, then demonstrate improvement later. Changing the marking guide creates a different baseline.

The percentage thresholds, calendar separation, timing comparisons, and work-sample rules are game decisions. They are not established clinical thresholds, and five-question forms are not validated as equally difficult. Results rely on self-report. An alternate is available when a test reaches its ceiling or no longer suits the user.

Existing characters retain all previously earned levels, base stats, purchases, and XP when exam gating is introduced. Their next future five-level milestone requires an exam. New characters use the exam rules from registration. Exams certify milestone points; an ordinary performance check does not directly add base stats.

## Classes and skills

All nonbasic classes require above-average stats. Advanced paths can be available from exceptional starting results, sometimes combined with credentials or a profession. Prestige and Ascendant paths also require earlier class levels. [Full class register](../class-register.md).

Fighter has 28 shared nodes, including four-step sword, spear, axe, and unarmed routes. Other archetypes have 12 shared nodes; every class also has four focused nodes. Independent branches and alternative prerequisites permit cross-training. Career trees have wider routes too. Purchases remain permanent and points, levels, and practice gates still apply. Class signatures and learned combat skills are usable in Adventures; career skills remain non-combat.

## Requests, gold, and notifications

Daily tasks are individual practice. Weekly quests can involve partners or groups: hiking, listening, study, service, and paired technique. A group hike splits training focus 70% CON / 30% CHA. Partial class/career credit follows the mix; total player XP is awarded once.

Requests award gold once per calendar period alongside XP. F–SSS gold rewards are **2 / 5 / 12 / 30 / 70 / 160 / 360 / 840 / 2,000**. Gold joins the existing Adventures wallet for supplies and equipment. Old completions do not receive retroactive gold, and spending does not cause already-settled rewards to return.

The fixed header's notification inbox shows recorded rewards, progression, and ready exams. Read state is saved. These are in-app notifications, not operating-system push reminders.
