# Version 1.14.0 — roadmap batch 1

[ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md) now holds the product priorities, stable IDs, acceptance checks, guardrails and delivery history. This release addresses **P01 skill progression** and a first **P02 Briar Road encounter pass**. Daily request flow, ordinary activity logging, account/device reliability, durable plans, career utility and the wider economy/endgame remain on the backlog.

## Ranked techniques

Current combat skills have shared effect profiles. Higher ranks improve damage, healing, barriers, counters and utility; MP costs and equipped combat modifiers are shown consistently in skill details and the Adventure loadout.

For example, **Spark** uses 5 MP at rank 1. **Spark: Control** uses 4 MP with 115% effect strength and +2 equipped combat INT; **Spark: Refinement** uses 4 MP with 140% effect strength and +3 combat INT. These modifiers apply only in Adventures.

Combat CHA above 10 now strengthens Rally and Formation bonuses. This gives Bard stat growth and CHA equipment/skills an effect on party support. Ordinary real-world attributes are unchanged by using these abilities.

Repeated effects within a shared archetype path, class focus, universal attribute path or opening technique form a family. Learning a stronger owned rank automatically upgrades an equipped earlier version and uses **one technique slot**. The loadout lists the strongest learned version of each family. Different weapon moves and separate technique families remain separate choices.

Earlier skill IDs, prerequisite rules, recorded point costs and ownership remain. No refund or extra charge occurs during slot consolidation. A character that had several versions equipped may have fewer occupied slots and different temporary combat modifiers afterward. Earlier versions stay visible in their skill history. Existing active battles retain their saved units and abilities; the updated loadout applies to new encounters.

## Opening encounters

The first two Briar Road stages stay simple. Stage three introduces **shells**: ordinary hits wear them down with reduced damage, while power, sweeping, magic, piercing and bash techniques break them. Analysis also removes a shell. Later stages introduce winding-up attacks and mixed enemies.

The guardian telegraphs a party-wide **Briar Cleave** every third round. Guarding, party barriers, healing and supplies can keep allies standing. Mira now has **Glimmer**, a light attack spell, alongside Restore so support-focused characters can use the companion offensively.

New tactics are saved with battle state and survive reload. Other regions retain their existing encounters. More enemy identities, target-intent detail and broader difficulty continuity remain part of P02; high-stat challenges and a complete economy pass remain P08.

The phone skill panel now keeps its selected star in the visible map area. The navigation menu opens above the panel, and battle/loadout utility labels have stronger contrast.

## Balance probes

The repeatable opening simulation compares eight basic classes with Attack-only and tactical policies, using baseline attributes of 10, Mira, initial equipment and three potions. No learned player skills or additional real-world XP are supplied. All eight tactical fixtures clear all ten stages; Attack-only fixtures clear nine and lose at the guardian. These results establish available approaches for the fixtures, not a universal difficulty guarantee or human usability validation.
