# Version 1.23.0

**Adventures → Guild requests → Elite hunts** adds 32 optional fights: Veteran, Elite, Mythic and Calamity in each of eight regions. Clear the region’s ten-stage outer route to unlock them. Inspect one selected hunt, its enemies, tactics and rewards before entering.

- Enemy strength is fixed by region and selected grade. Normal campaign enemies remain unchanged.
- Ironhide reforms armor, Bloodtrail targets wounded allies, Mana Reaver pressures MP, and Frenzy enters a stronger wounded phase. Their intentions are visible in battle.
- Hunt rewards provide gold and local brewing/smith materials. They never grant real-world XP or base stats.
- **Hunt again** claims an unpaid victory once and immediately repeats the fight with current reserves. **Hunt board** claims once and returns to the selected hunt. Retreats, reloads and failed saves retain the existing resource and transaction rules.
- Companions keep their original signatures and player-level growth. Orin adds party protection, Lyra piercing arrows, Gideon armor analysis, Nessa group healing, Toma burning and Elara guarded counters. Mira keeps Restore and Glimmer. New commands appear in new battles; older saved battles remain intact.

Companion-led builds remain valid for support- and career-focused characters, as requested. No companion stats are reduced. Automatic full recovery remains local midnight; discovery/ambush state, request rewards, supply prices, tempering costs and guild-rank thresholds remain.

The [combat report](hunts-1.23/README.md) contains 1,568 synthetic fixtures covering classes, regions, fixed grades, tactics, supplies, learned loadouts, focused stats and companion growth. These are independent combat snapshots, not another earned-account or daily-economy study.
