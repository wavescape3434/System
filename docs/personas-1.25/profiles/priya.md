# Priya Desai — Firefighter

**Fictional customer; scripted app outcomes. The experience assessment below is an inference, not a testimonial.**

## Background

Priya already follows an established fitness programme and enjoys martial JRPG builds. She wants to record controlled gym sessions and review emergency planning using fictional exercises. She is not using the app to obtain operational clearance. Her starting assessment is deliberately strong, and she has enough scheduled time to attempt harder requests without making every day a maximum test.

## Goals and intended use

- **Scenario career output:** A fictional tabletop exercise with communication and debrief notes.
- **Learning focus:** Preparedness and after-action review.
- **Class ambition:** Champion. The chosen class is independent of the profession.
- **Constraints:** Training, career practice and Adventures share a limited leisure budget; ordinary work is not automatically submitted for XP.
