# Paige Sato — Career Changer

**Fictional customer; scripted app outcomes. The experience assessment below is an inference, not a testimonial.**

## Background

Paige is deciding what to do after leaving a degree programme. She wants small completed work samples and a sense of direction, not a character that judges the decision. The scenario gives her a later move into design practice as an input, not a job outcome caused by the app. Her first two workout attempts are incomplete, making partial-work handling visible.

## Goals and intended use

- **Scenario career output:** A fictional small design brief with two revisions.
- **Learning focus:** Exploratory creative work samples.
- **Class ambition:** Maestro. The chosen class is independent of the profession.
- **Constraints:** Uncertain schedule, incomplete early workouts, and a deliberate change of professional direction.

## Experience assessment

The model treats two incomplete strength attempts as unfinished work and later preserves the career change. That is accurate reward behavior, but it also exposes the current absence of a separate earned-progress record for partial sessions. Her later career focus can grow while Bard remains largely untrained. The detailed creative assignments are authored inputs; the app’s generic career-changer request is less specific than its Analyst job-application branch.

**2 incomplete workout attempts** earned no receipt or XP. The simulation records those attempts in its trace; the current app does not create independent Journey entries from an unfinished workout draft.

Current aspiration: **Maestro**. Remaining requirements: CHA 36; Bard Lv. 25; Maestro entry trial.
