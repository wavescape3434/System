# Evan Park — Accountant

**Fictional customer; scripted app outcomes. The experience assessment below is an inference, not a testimonial.**

## Background

Evan is a working accountant with two young children. He likes careful systems and turn-based strategy, but a tax-season deadline makes an uninterrupted annual streak implausible. He wants to improve his spreadsheet checks and resume short walks without turning family time into another quota. Archmage is a long-term aspiration rather than a promised reward for logging office hours.

## Goals and intended use

- **Scenario career output:** A fictional ledger reconciled with an explicit exception log.
- **Learning focus:** Reconciliation and error checking.
- **Class ambition:** Archmage. The chosen class is independent of the profession.
- **Constraints:** School pickups, a deadline-season break, and at most 45 planned real-world minutes.
