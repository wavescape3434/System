# Ruth Okafor — Farmer

**Fictional customer; scripted app outcomes. The experience assessment below is an inference, not a testimonial.**

## Background

Ruth runs a small vegetable farm with her spouse. She already moves throughout the day, but wants deliberate mobility practice and a clearer record of irrigation experiments. Harvest leaves little time for a phone. She enjoys exploring natural regions and gathering useful materials more than collecting every combat skill. Her planned seasonal absences are explicit inputs, not a prediction about farmers.

## Goals and intended use

- **Scenario career output:** A plot-observation sheet comparing two irrigation schedules.
- **Learning focus:** Irrigation observation and seasonal planning.
- **Class ambition:** Beastwarden. The chosen class is independent of the profession.
- **Constraints:** Training, career practice and Adventures share a limited leisure budget; ordinary work is not automatically submitted for XP.

## Experience assessment

Seasonal availability matters more than a daily streak. Her gathered-resource tool fits the farming fantasy, while the model retains all earlier work through harvest. A plan that shows which quests fit the remaining season would serve her better than treating every calendar period as interchangeable.
