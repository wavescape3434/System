# Irene Patel — Librarian

**Fictional customer; scripted app outcomes. The experience assessment below is an inference, not a testimonial.**

## Background

Irene runs library learning sessions and wants to study both source evaluation and Christian reflection. She likes the Guild’s record of retained classes, and deliberately spends a month as a Ranger to focus on outdoor activity. She returns to scholarship afterward. The model checks whether that detour helps, delays or obscures her long-term Sage goal.

## Goals and intended use

- **Scenario career output:** A fictional source-comparison lesson with retrieval checks.
- **Learning focus:** Source evaluation and accessible teaching.
- **Class ambition:** Sage. The chosen class is independent of the profession.
- **Constraints:** Training, career practice and Adventures share a limited leisure budget; ordinary work is not automatically submitted for XP.

## Experience assessment

Her outdoor detour retains earlier scholarship and adds another class record. Returning to a Sage goal can leave several exam/lineage steps open at once. A chronological Journey is useful here, but a concise current-next-step plan would make the preserved history easier to act on.

Current aspiration: **Sage**. Its stated requirements were met; the class was earned through the corresponding trial.
