# Hugo Laurent — Chef

**Fictional customer; scripted app outcomes. The experience assessment below is an inference, not a testimonial.**

## Background

Hugo leads a restaurant kitchen and wants deliberate strength training rather than counting a long shift as a workout. He also studies menu costing and food-service systems. He wants a fighter who balances muscular strength with consistent Christian Scripture practice. His schedule is busiest at weekends, so this account does not receive the usual Saturday gaming window.

## Goals and intended use

- **Scenario career output:** A fictional menu-cost worksheet and a service-quality review.
- **Learning focus:** Menu costing and service quality.
- **Class ambition:** Paladin. The chosen class is independent of the profession.
- **Constraints:** Training, career practice and Adventures share a limited leisure budget; ordinary work is not automatically submitted for XP.

## Experience assessment

Paladin requires both the named predecessor levels and higher STR/WIS values. His kitchen career mainly grows DEX, so those requirements are not automatically satisfied by professional progress. The long route is real in this scenario; meeting the class-level prerequisites alone is not the end of the promotion.

Current aspiration: **Paladin**. Remaining requirements: STR 28; WIS 25; Paladin entry trial.
