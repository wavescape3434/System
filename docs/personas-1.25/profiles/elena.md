# Elena Cruz — Baker

**Fictional customer; scripted app outcomes. The experience assessment below is an inference, not a testimonial.**

## Background

Elena works early bakery shifts and is building a design portfolio in the afternoons. Halfway through the scenario she changes profession and vocation in the app to match that new focus. No employment result is inferred. She wants old baking progress to remain visible while her current career receives the prominent level and skill tree.

## Goals and intended use

- **Scenario career output:** A fictional product sheet, first as a service artifact and later as a design portfolio sample.
- **Learning focus:** Documented product presentation and design revision.
- **Class ambition:** Eldritch Knight. The chosen class is independent of the profession.
- **Constraints:** Training, career practice and Adventures share a limited leisure budget; ordinary work is not automatically submitted for XP.

## Experience assessment

The career change keeps Baker history while giving Graphic Designer its own level and a second utility. The new role level is lower than the no-change counterfactual, as expected when practice is split between careers. The app should present that as retained breadth rather than erased progress.

Current aspiration: **Eldritch Knight**. Remaining requirements: STR 28; Eldritch Knight entry trial.
