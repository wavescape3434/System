## Background

Mei builds financial models and is saving time for evening study. She likes elemental magic and wants her game choices to reflect focused learning. Her success measure is a defensible forecast and checked assumptions, not a higher IQ label. She begins with a strong result on the app’s small reasoning check; that is a scripted game result, not a professional norm.
