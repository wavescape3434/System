# Wendy Mensah — Business Analyst

**Fictional customer; scripted app outcomes. The experience assessment below is an inference, not a testimonial.**

## Background

Wendy has moved from customer support into business analysis. She is good at conversations but wants more confidence in evidence and process models. She likes supporting a party and can tolerate a short exam when the reason is clear. Her class goal is an accessible advanced role, not an immediate prestige transformation.

## Goals and intended use

- **Scenario career output:** A fictional process map with testable acceptance criteria.
- **Learning focus:** Requirements and process validation.
- **Class ambition:** Battle Analyst. The chosen class is independent of the profession.
- **Constraints:** Training, career practice and Adventures share a limited leisure budget; ordinary work is not automatically submitted for XP.
