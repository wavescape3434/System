# Ada Bennett — Teacher

**Fictional customer; scripted app outcomes. The experience assessment below is an inference, not a testimonial.**

## Background

Ada teaches primary school and leads a small church choir. School preparation often consumes her evenings, so she wants a small teaching artifact to count more than a vague productivity timer. She likes JRPG support roles and wants a Maestro eventually. The model gives her a school-break gap rather than assuming that teachers maintain identical routines all year.

## Goals and intended use

- **Scenario career output:** A short phonemic-awareness lesson with an answer check and one revision.
- **Learning focus:** Early-reading retrieval practice.
- **Class ambition:** Maestro. The chosen class is independent of the profession.
- **Constraints:** Training, career practice and Adventures share a limited leisure budget; ordinary work is not automatically submitted for XP.

## Experience assessment

Teaching preparation is rewarded strongly, but a high player/profession level does not substitute for Bard class work. Her Maestro aspiration can remain distant despite an impressive guild card. The app needs to show the next relevant class commitment, rather than only recommending more work in the already fast-growing career.

Current aspiration: **Maestro**. Remaining requirements: Bard Lv. 25; Maestro entry trial.
