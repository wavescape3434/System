# Owen Price — Truck Driver

**Fictional customer; scripted app outcomes. The experience assessment below is an inference, not a testimonial.**

## Background

Owen drives regional freight routes and has little control over where an evening ends. He wants short walks and better route-planning habits, not an aggressive lifting challenge. His phone is his main device. He is interested in a durable defender, but mostly wants the app to make small weeks feel like retained progress.

## Goals and intended use

- **Scenario career output:** A fictional load-check sheet and route-risk summary.
- **Learning focus:** Inventory accuracy and route handoffs.
- **Class ambition:** Ironheart. The chosen class is independent of the profession.
- **Constraints:** Uncertain stops, four app days and a 25-minute cap.

## Experience assessment

His short windows still produce retained progress, but splitting them across conditioning and career work misses many calendar thresholds. That is a schedule/reward issue to inspect, not evidence that the work is worthless. The logistics tool is available once he deliberately reviews skills on a day he actually uses the app.

Current aspiration: **Ironheart**. Remaining requirements: CON 34; STR 25; Sentinel Lv. 25; Ironheart entry trial.
