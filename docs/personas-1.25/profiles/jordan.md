# Jordan Foster — IT Support Specialist

**Fictional customer; scripted app outcomes. The experience assessment below is an inference, not a testimonial.**

## Background

Jordan has several years of desktop-support experience and wants to move toward network operations. He studies in a small home lab, but also wants to become less sedentary. The scenario explicitly assumes a Network+ result in month six; the app only records that claim and does not verify the credential. This is one technical persona among a much broader cohort.

## Goals and intended use

- **Scenario career output:** A documented virtual network lab with a failure/recovery test.
- **Learning focus:** Switching, routing and fault isolation.
- **Class ambition:** Network Weaver. The chosen class is independent of the profession.
- **Constraints:** Training, career practice and Adventures share a limited leisure budget; ordinary work is not automatically submitted for XP.
