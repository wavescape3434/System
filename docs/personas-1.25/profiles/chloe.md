# Chloe Nguyen — Quality Control Inspector

**Fictional customer; scripted app outcomes. The experience assessment below is an inference, not a testimonial.**

## Background

Chloe works an early manufacturing shift and studies measurement after work. She wants evidence that she is becoming more careful, rather than rewards for speed alone. She likes precise combat and short sessions. A night-course block changes her availability halfway through the year, which lets the study distinguish stored progress from continuous attendance.

## Goals and intended use

- **Scenario career output:** A fictional inspection log with tolerance checks and an error review.
- **Learning focus:** Measurement repeatability and defect classification.
- **Class ambition:** Duelist. The chosen class is independent of the profession.
- **Constraints:** Training, career practice and Adventures share a limited leisure budget; ordinary work is not automatically submitted for XP.

## Experience assessment

Measurement and quality work have their own deliverable and utility. Duelist gives an early focused identity while her inspection career continues independently. The game should keep accuracy and reviewed work prominent, rather than reward faster tapping as if it proved manufacturing competence.

Current aspiration: **Duelist**. Its stated requirements were met; the class was earned through the corresponding trial.
