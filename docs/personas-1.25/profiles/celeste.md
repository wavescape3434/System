# Celeste Morgan — Project Manager

**Fictional customer; scripted app outcomes. The experience assessment below is an inference, not a testimonial.**

## Background

Celeste delivers public-sector projects and has returned to gaming after a long break. She wants stronger risk reviews and clearer facilitation, while keeping her own exercise plan modest. Guild Strategist appeals to her, but she expects the app to show why a promotion remains locked. Her model includes both technical and communication practice.

## Goals and intended use

- **Scenario career output:** A fictional project charter, risk register and retrospective.
- **Learning focus:** Risk registers and acceptance criteria.
- **Class ambition:** Guild Strategist. The chosen class is independent of the profession.
- **Constraints:** Training, career practice and Adventures share a limited leisure budget; ordinary work is not automatically submitted for XP.
