# Nadia Silva — Administrative Assistant

**Fictional customer; scripted app outcomes. The experience assessment below is an inference, not a testimonial.**

## Background

Nadia manages scheduling for a busy office and has a toddler at home. She wants fewer handoff errors, a short walk and a clear next action when she opens the app. She likes the guild-card presentation but is not an experienced gamer. Her limited schedule tests whether modest, reliable work feels worthwhile beside more active users.

## Goals and intended use

- **Scenario career output:** A conflict-checked fictional rota and a revised handoff checklist.
- **Learning focus:** Scheduling and accurate handoffs.
- **Class ambition:** Warden. The chosen class is independent of the profession.
- **Constraints:** Childcare, four app days and a 30-minute real-world budget.

## Experience assessment

This is a viable career-first player with a nearly untouched combat class. The schedule usually spends the available block on vocation work, leaving no time for Sentinel practice. The UI should show that deliberate tradeoff and banked class XP; a class level of 1 does not mean the account has made no progress.
