# Maya Ortiz — Registered Nurse

**Fictional customer; scripted app outcomes. The experience assessment below is an inference, not a testimonial.**

## Background

Maya works three twelve-hour hospital shifts and shares an apartment with her sister. She likes supporting a party more than dealing damage, but she has abandoned streak apps after a difficult week. She wants a record of teach-back practice and gentle conditioning that survives a rota change. The care work in this scenario uses fictional cases; patient records never enter the app.

## Goals and intended use

- **Scenario career output:** A fictional discharge teach-back script checked against a role-appropriate rubric.
- **Learning focus:** Structured handoff and teach-back.
- **Class ambition:** Healer. The chosen class is independent of the profession.
- **Constraints:** Rotating shifts, four planned app days, no patient information, and one disrupted fortnight.
