# Graham Ellis — Research Assistant

**Fictional customer; scripted app outcomes. The experience assessment below is an inference, not a testimonial.**

## Background

Graham works in a university research group while applying for postgraduate study. He wants to distinguish reading a paper from understanding its limitations. He enjoys scholarship and Christian reflection, but his ambition to become a Sage requires genuine progress in two class histories. Conference travel and a deliberately modest budget prevent a perfect, optimized playthrough.

## Goals and intended use

- **Scenario career output:** A reproducible analysis of an ethical public sample dataset.
- **Learning focus:** Reproducibility and uncertainty.
- **Class ambition:** Sage. The chosen class is independent of the profession.
- **Constraints:** Training, career practice and Adventures share a limited leisure budget; ordinary work is not automatically submitted for XP.
