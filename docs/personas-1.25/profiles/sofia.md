# Sofia Marin — Paralegal

**Fictional customer; scripted app outcomes. The experience assessment below is an inference, not a testimonial.**

## Background

Sofia works in a legal aid office and spends much of her day organizing evidence. She likes precision-focused characters and wants to improve concise drafting and public speaking. She cannot put client information into a personal app. The modeled outputs therefore use invented cases. Her class and profession emphasize different attributes, making this a useful test of a mixed focus.

## Goals and intended use

- **Scenario career output:** An indexed fictional case brief with source and jurisdiction limits.
- **Learning focus:** Source hierarchy and issue spotting.
- **Class ambition:** Tactician. The chosen class is independent of the profession.
- **Constraints:** No client records; occasional late work and a strict 50-minute budget.
