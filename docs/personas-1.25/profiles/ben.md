# Ben Wallace — Sales Representative

**Fictional customer; scripted app outcomes. The experience assessment below is an inference, not a testimonial.**

## Background

Ben travels for a business-to-business sales role and coaches a youth sports team. He wants to practice listening before presenting an offer, and to keep a small hotel-room workout. He enjoys directing a party but does not want a networking syllabus. Travel makes manual weekly acceptance easy to miss, even when he has done useful work.

## Goals and intended use

- **Scenario career output:** A fictional discovery-call outline and evidence-based proposal.
- **Learning focus:** Discovery questions and honest proposals.
- **Class ambition:** Marshal. The chosen class is independent of the profession.
- **Constraints:** Travel, a 40-minute cap, and inconsistent calendar commitments.

## Experience assessment

The travel pattern exposes the cost of commitments that assume a predictable week. His listening quest is a separate modeled group activity, not another claim for the same sales block. A short, actionable return screen would be more useful than a streak warning after travel.

Current aspiration: **Marshal**. Its stated requirements were met; the class was earned through the corresponding trial.
