# Daniel Cho — Operations Manager

**Fictional customer; scripted app outcomes. The experience assessment below is an inference, not a testimonial.**

## Background

Daniel manages warehouse operations and shares school pickups with his partner. He wants to reduce recurring process mistakes and make conditioning consistent. He prefers practical guild tools to a large combat rotation. An operations vocation should fit his actual work without forcing him into analysis or networking content.

## Goals and intended use

- **Scenario career output:** A fictional process map with a capacity check and one tested improvement.
- **Learning focus:** Process handoffs and capacity planning.
- **Class ambition:** Marshal. The chosen class is independent of the profession.
- **Constraints:** Training, career practice and Adventures share a limited leisure budget; ordinary work is not automatically submitted for XP.
