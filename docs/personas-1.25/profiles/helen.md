# Helen Ward — Explorer

**Fictional customer; scripted app outcomes. The experience assessment below is an inference, not a testimonial.**

## Background

Helen is retired from bookkeeping and helps organize a church reading group. She is comfortable with a phone but new to games. She wants a gentle walking routine and a clear record of what she has learned; the fantasy layer is optional for her. Adventures are limited to one weekend encounter, so an excellent combat build is not her main success criterion.

## Goals and intended use

- **Scenario career output:** A sourced local-history note distinguishing evidence from memory.
- **Learning focus:** Local-history sources and practical learning.
- **Class ambition:** Scripture Scribe. The chosen class is independent of the profession.
- **Constraints:** A 25-minute routine, large-text preference and little familiarity with RPG terminology.
