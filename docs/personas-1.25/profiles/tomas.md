# Tomas Vega — Electrician

**Fictional customer; scripted app outcomes. The experience assessment below is an inference, not a testimonial.**

## Background

Tomas is a licensed electrician who supervises one apprentice and helps care for his father. He enjoys building things but has little interest in networking certificates. He wants his character to reflect fault finding, measurement and a steady return to lifting. His professional practice is a de-energized training example or drawing, not an app-directed live electrical repair.

## Goals and intended use

- **Scenario career output:** A checked training-board fault tree and labeled circuit drawing.
- **Learning focus:** Measurement and fault isolation.
- **Class ambition:** Grand Artificer. The chosen class is independent of the profession.
- **Constraints:** Family care interrupts two evenings; energized electrical work is outside this simulation.

## Experience assessment

This is a meaningful non-IT technical path: electrical learning unlocks the trades tool while class work develops Artificer. His professional DEX exam and the INT focus of his class are different checks. The app should explain that split and should never imply that a game level licenses electrical work.


At year end the recorded metrics also qualify this character to take the entry trial for **World Forger**. The scenario stops at its declared goal and does not automatically choose that class.

Current aspiration: **Grand Artificer**. Its stated requirements were met; the class was earned through the corresponding trial.
