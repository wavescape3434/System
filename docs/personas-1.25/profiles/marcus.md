# Marcus Reed — Social Worker

**Fictional customer; scripted app outcomes. The experience assessment below is an inference, not a testimonial.**

## Background

Marcus coordinates community referrals and volunteers at his church. He wants reflection to result in better listening and a useful resource directory, not just longer reading sessions. He likes healing roles but is new to RPG systems. A low-intensity opening and fictional case notes let the simulation examine the app outside its technical-career comfort zone.

## Goals and intended use

- **Scenario career output:** A fictional referral plan with eligibility limits and consent checks.
- **Learning focus:** Boundaries and resource mapping.
- **Class ambition:** High Cleric. The chosen class is independent of the profession.
- **Constraints:** Training, career practice and Adventures share a limited leisure budget; ordinary work is not automatically submitted for XP.

## Experience assessment

Community work, faith practice and a healer identity fit the app’s mission closely. The monthly service event is an explicitly assumed group activity. A stronger class and large WIS value should reward persistence without presenting spiritual maturity or professional judgment as a validated numerical score.

Current aspiration: **High Cleric**. Its stated requirements were met; the class was earned through the corresponding trial.
