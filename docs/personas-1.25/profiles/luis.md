# Luis Alvarez — Physical Therapist

**Fictional customer; scripted app outcomes. The experience assessment below is an inference, not a testimonial.**

## Background

Luis is an experienced clinician and recreational martial artist. He starts with a capable movement assessment but wants to improve patient explanations using fictional cases. He wants to develop a Warpriest through strength and Christian faith practice rather than chase damage alone. The scenario includes a period away from the app and tests whether a higher starting class removes the need for later exams—it should not.

## Goals and intended use

- **Scenario career output:** A fictional exercise explanation with teach-back checks.
- **Learning focus:** Clear explanations and evidence appraisal.
- **Class ambition:** Warpriest. The chosen class is independent of the profession.
- **Constraints:** Training, career practice and Adventures share a limited leisure budget; ordinary work is not automatically submitted for XP.
