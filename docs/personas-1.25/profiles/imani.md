# Imani Brooks — Graphic Designer

**Fictional customer; scripted app outcomes. The experience assessment below is an inference, not a testimonial.**

## Background

Imani is a freelance designer who works irregular hours and dances for fun. She wants a place to see revisions and movement practice accumulate without treating every commission as a competition. She is comfortable choosing a class for its style and switching later. Two quiet freelance weeks reduce her available routine rather than magically producing extra game time.

## Goals and intended use

- **Scenario career output:** A fictional event poster with a before/after critique.
- **Learning focus:** Visual hierarchy and revision.
- **Class ambition:** Minstrel. The chosen class is independent of the profession.
- **Constraints:** Training, career practice and Adventures share a limited leisure budget; ordinary work is not automatically submitted for XP.
