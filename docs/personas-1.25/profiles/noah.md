# Noah Williams — Christian Ministry Worker

**Fictional customer; scripted app outcomes. The experience assessment below is an inference, not a testimonial.**

## Background

Noah coordinates youth activities and service volunteers at his church. He wants Scripture comprehension to connect with thoughtful planning and listening. He likes a healer identity and has time for consistent practice, but does not want the game to call a higher WIS score greater spiritual worth. A family holiday interrupts the year without becoming a penalty.

## Goals and intended use

- **Scenario career output:** A fictional youth-service plan with boundaries, context and a review.
- **Learning focus:** Service planning and Scripture context.
- **Class ambition:** High Cleric. The chosen class is independent of the profession.
- **Constraints:** Training, career practice and Adventures share a limited leisure budget; ordinary work is not automatically submitted for XP.

## Experience assessment

This is a sustained faith-and-service route with a High Cleric identity and useful community work. Group service is separate from solo daily tasks. Large WIS values are RPG progression; the app should continue to keep spiritual worth outside the scoring claim.
