# Aisha Rahman — Software Developer

**Fictional customer; scripted app outcomes. The experience assessment below is an inference, not a testimonial.**

## Background

Aisha works on a small application team and wants to replace unstructured tutorial watching with tested implementations. She likes frost magic and early specialization. Her character should earn relevant skills through work and class practice rather than receive a developer-themed stat bonus. A release crunch interrupts her routine for two weeks.

## Goals and intended use

- **Scenario career output:** A small fictional feature with normal, edge and recovery checks.
- **Learning focus:** Testing and failure isolation.
- **Class ambition:** Arcanist → Archmage. The chosen class is independent of the profession.
- **Constraints:** Training, career practice and Adventures share a limited leisure budget; ordinary work is not automatically submitted for XP.

## Experience assessment
