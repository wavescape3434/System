# Leila Haddad — Architect

**Fictional customer; scripted app outcomes. The experience assessment below is an inference, not a testimonial.**

## Background

Leila has recently joined a small architecture practice. She wants to get better at explaining design choices, not merely finish more drawings. She is already a fantasy-game fan and prefers an Artificer identity to a generic office-avatar. Her sample project is a fictional room layout with dimensions and accessibility checks; the app does not approve a real building design.

## Goals and intended use

- **Scenario career output:** A fictional layout, alternatives and a dimension-check sheet.
- **Learning focus:** Design constraints and drawing checks.
- **Class ambition:** Engineer. The chosen class is independent of the profession.
- **Constraints:** Training, career practice and Adventures share a limited leisure budget; ordinary work is not automatically submitted for XP.

## Experience assessment

Engineer is a successful advanced identity, but its high level does not satisfy Grand Artificer’s Artificer-level prerequisite. This exposes a vertical-progression design question: should relevant advanced training count toward a lineage, or should the UI clearly recommend returning to the predecessor class?

Current aspiration: **Engineer**. Its stated requirements were met; the class was earned through the corresponding trial.
