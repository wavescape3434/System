# Method and assumptions

This study asks what the **current 1.25.0 app rules** do for a broad set of authored customers. It is an executable scenario study, not a real-user pilot, an occupational benchmark or a forecast of fitness, faith, employment or learning outcomes.

## Coverage and time

- **32 fictional adult profiles**, ages 21–68, with **32 distinct starting job titles**. They cover all **20 maintained profession categories** and **25 selectable vocations**, not just analysis and networking.
- **35 annual scenarios**: the 32 primary profiles plus a review-policy alternative for Theo, a no-career-change alternative for Elena, and a no-Adventures alternative for Maya. Alternatives reuse the same person and attendance draws.
- Each runs **365 local calendar days**, from **1 February 2027 through 31 January 2028**, at local noon. Checkpoints are days **7, 30, 90, 180 and 365**. Profile time zones include America/Denver, Europe/London and Asia/Singapore; applicable DST and year changes use the actual calendar functions.
- Total: **12,775 account-days**. The 140-day smoke test, development pilots, isolated mechanics probes and repeatability replay are separate from that total.
- The cohort is deliberately varied, not sampled from a market. Ages, professions and backgrounds do not establish demand, retention rates or differences between demographic groups. Broad job coverage does not make this app a validated training tool for all those jobs.

## What is fictional input

Backstories, available minutes, chosen goals, routines, absences, work quality, exercise performance, exam results, review habits and player choices are authored inputs. The named career assignments are scenario examples; the app currently supplies category guidance, not an automatic individualized curriculum for every job title. They are not inferred from a person's age, name or occupation.

Physical fixtures describe an introductory, steady, trained, chair-based or unmeasured starting scenario. They use a common 170 cm / 75 kg body-size fixture and an unspecified reference-sex selection, rather than assuming a demographic norm from a name. Priya has an explicit stronger pushup input; Paige leaves the initial assessment unmeasured. These are game assessments, with the app's existing provisional references. The study is not a new population-norm calibration.

Mental registration fixtures use the app's actual forms with declared 3/5, 4/5 or 5/5 answer patterns and a scripted duration. New quiz exams request unused forms and submit declared answers through `beginExam` and `submitMentalExam`. No Bible questions are added. These short forms are authored game checks, not equated IQ or personality tests.

Work-sample exam scores follow a declared, saturating **50 → 72** illustrative curve based on recorded work for that **profession/category or class owner**. A newly promoted class starts practice against its own rubric. This is not a prediction that a real portfolio will improve at that rate. The profession sample counts matching-category work; a software vocation does not automatically improve the modeled creative-work rubric. Physical checks use same-protocol illustrative improvement curves and the app's alternate/maturity rules. After two qualifying improvements, the actor may choose the real mastery route. The script never writes `status: passed`, levels, XP or base-stat gains directly.

Only Jordan receives an assumed Network+ credential, in month six. It is recorded through the same character field used by the app's certificate form and awarded by `awardSkills`. It is explicitly a self-report scenario, not external certification verification. Other professional qualifications in backstories are context, not invented game certificates.

## Daily choices and time

Every customer has a stated priority and schedule. Available real-world minutes are checked each opened day; no day may exceed its declared budget. Attendance uses fixed per-person/day seeded draws and explicit breaks. **Attendance probability is an input**, not measured retention. No work or login reward is claimed on an absent day.

The actor proposes class work, profession practice, a separate vocation block and walking in its stated priority order. Blocks that do not fit are omitted. A typical ordinary class block is 20 minutes; profession and vocation practice are separate 20-minute blocks with distinct declared outputs. A non-trained fixture keeps a five-minute walking target; trained fixtures use the 30-minute brisk request. This intentionally includes modest routines, not universal attainment of the adult aerobic guideline. The model does not independently increase walking targets or forecast endurance from short walks.

Strength-primary classes follow the current A/B regimen on two scheduled days weekly. Logged work sets use the real prescription, blank-to-actual completion assumptions, initial lower-range then top-range reps, stated effort and explicit load inputs. On suitable review dates, a ready actor can manually choose a higher F–C plan rank if its time budget supports it. Hybrid class focus costs another five minutes. Loads are illustrative scenario choices independent of game STR; actual training quality or physiological response is not verified.

Paige's two below-minimum workouts are modeled as unfinished attempts: time is spent, no receipt or XP is awarded. They remain study trace events. The app's device-local draft storage is not itself exercised by this domain simulation; independent partial-session Journey entries remain missing.

Five profiles additionally schedule a distinct group hike, study circle, listening quest or Christian service event once per four-week block, with a larger stated weekend budget where needed. Group participation and feedback are assumed. No multiplayer or real group coordination service is exercised.

## Deliberate acceptance and goals

The script calls **Take** for each selected commitment; it never bypasses manual acceptance. A period-specific consent draw models whether that actor chooses the weekly/monthly request. It retries submission only after the real calendar, practice, equipment, overlap and linked-condition gates allow it. Completion counts are request receipts, not a count of independent real-world activities: legitimate daily work can also feed accepted calendar goals. Calendar reviews cost three modeled minutes. Project quality and separate weekly/monthly deliverables are assumed; calendar eligibility alone does not prove that a real project was completed.

Career-priority actors choose profession/vocation commitments first. Balanced actors choose class/vocation commitments; minimal actors choose walking commitments. These are scenario policies, not recommendations that every user take that exact set. Repeated missing-requirement checks count attempts, not distinct user frustrations. Expiry counts are unique uncompleted accepted calendar requests. No earned XP is removed by expiry or absence.

A **goal-aware scenario planner** inspects the app's class requirements, trains a missing predecessor, stays in a suitable current focus when possible and attempts a qualifying entry trial. This is a simulation convenience, not an automatic coaching feature claimed to exist in the UI. A real person must discover the path in class details. Basic/advanced trial work consumes 25 minutes; prestige trials receive three separate 15-minute preparation sessions before submission. That honors the written trial description even where dated three-session enforcement is absent.

Quinn deliberately retains the UX Designer profession while pursuing a software vocation. Elena and Paige have explicit career-change dates. Irene and Paige have declared class detours. These are choices, not predicted job offers, forced class changes or inferred user preferences.

## Exams, skills and combat

Review-policy actors reserve a ten-minute exam block on their last scheduled weekly app day, when a target is ready and its previous attempt is at least fourteen days old. Class reviews are prioritized before other ready targets. Theo's primary run deliberately refuses exams. Work-sample routes, fresh mental forms, baseline/improvement/failed/mastery statuses and the five-level gates all run through the actual APIs. Large banked XP can coexist with a low certified level. Explicit monthly stat rewards remain possible without exam passes.

Actors deliberately review skills on the next opened day at least seven days after their previous review. They buy at most three eligible nodes per review, prioritizing named goal prerequisites, relevant career roots, active-class focus and a declared combat style. This is **not an app purchase cap**. All purchases spend real earned wallet points and satisfy the real prerequisites. Up to four learned techniques are equipped; class signatures remain active-class abilities.

Adventures occur only on each person's chosen days and at their chosen encounter budget. The shared existing `economyAction` heuristic heals, guards against charges, uses an affordable ability or attacks. It is not a model of each person's tactical skill and does not systematically optimize elemental matchups or the preferred loadout. Outcomes therefore do not establish comparative class/build strength.

Normal campaign stages, occasional cleared-area exploration and available roaming bosses use the actual world functions. The bot returns to the map/shops between outings for preparation; this is a scenario policy, not a requirement of the continuous-field UI. Companions are recruited only after their real UI unlock and at most two are selected. Actual gold/materials buy supplies and useful current-town gear; the policy tempers to at most +2 and can brew available Greater supplies. No gear, stock, party member, materials or currency is injected. Relevant delivery materials retain a small reserve. Reports note availability of career tools; the model does not measure opening those panels.

There are no Elite hunts in this cohort. HP/MP persist across encounters; the app's local-midnight recovery remains. The actor does not get a full heal between fights. This study does not replace the earlier dedicated Elite or 3–5-encounter-per-day economy studies.

## Verification and provenance

- App version **1.25.0**, the app source commit/tree and immutable source hashes are in [results.json](results.json). No production app rule or APK is changed by this study.
- Every day checks the full gold ledger against request/login/battle/exploration/commission income minus actual spending. Every Adventure session asserts unchanged real-world XP and stats.
- Weekly, at each named checkpoint and at the final day, characters undergo full schema validation and JSON round-trip validation. This is not a claim of a new native-storage test on all 12,775 days.
- Every 47th completed request is deliberately submitted again and must return the unchanged character: **479 duplicate guards** passed. All battles finish below the 180-action bound.
- The Maya counterfactual must match all five real-world progression checkpoints exactly. Final wallets reconcile for every scenario. An independent 365-day Leila replay produced byte-identical case JSON and daily CSV: [repeatability record](repeatability.json).
- Seven final [character fixtures](accounts/README.md) are included and validate. They contain 2027–2028 timestamps for this fictional calendar; they are inspection/reproduction records, not current-date demo accounts. Importing a future-dated record can defer calendar/login/reset behavior until its recorded dates. Other customers have per-profile raw traces and daily CSVs.
- Supplementary [mechanics probes](mechanics-probes.json) inspect all 51 class daily scopes, 32 profession-request stat labels, cross-category credit, a declared bounded-rubric plateau and seven saved accounts' skill wallets. These isolated probes are not extra customers.

## Reproduce

Use the project’s Node/npm dependencies and Python 3 with Matplotlib and NumPy for the figures. The current cloud environment has these available.

```sh
node scripts/simulate-personas.mjs --smoke
node scripts/simulate-personas.mjs
node scripts/merge-personas.mjs --from-single
node scripts/probe-persona-findings.mjs
python3 scripts/report-personas.py
python3 scripts/verify-personas.py
```

For faster independent batches, run `--batch=0/4`, `1/4`, `2/4` and `3/4`, then `node scripts/merge-personas.mjs`. `--resume` only accepts completed cases with matching code/input fingerprints and duration. `--profile=leila --days=365` reruns one primary scenario; a single-profile run writes a single-profile `results.json`, so rebuild the cohort with `node scripts/simulate-personas.mjs --resume` and `node scripts/merge-personas.mjs --from-single` before generating a report. Intermediate batch partitions are ignored by Git; the merged cohort and individual case records are published.

These are domain-level models. They do not test human discovery of controls, touch/keyboards, notification delivery, native lifecycle, Google linking, clinical efficacy, actual work samples or customer satisfaction. The best next research step is a small, varied real-user pilot with observed task completion and clear consent; the best next engineering step is the reproduced class-scope defect.
