# Version 1.22.0

The town shop now has an **Apothecary** and a **Tempering** bench. Gold and materials can improve the supplies and equipment you use in Adventures.

- Brew Greater potions/ethers in any accessible town. Adept or better towns also brew Royal supplies. Preview whole-batch gold and local material costs before spending; brewing uses your materials rather than general-store stock.
- Greater supplies restore at least 150 HP / 60 MP or 25% of maximum. Royal supplies restore at least 450 HP / 180 MP or 50% of maximum. Recovery stops at full; fallen allies cannot consume them. Use carried brews from Supplies or during a battle turn.
- Temper owned town equipment at its original smith, up to +5. Each rank improves its original combat modifier; gold costs double and material costs rise. Preview the current and next modifier before spending. Item upgrades persist when unequipped and in backups, without raising base stats or restoring reserves.
- Delivery sheets include brewing and upgrade material trade-offs. Trades appraisals include tempered modifiers; Healthcare triage includes carried brews.
- Failed saves roll back gold, materials, inventory, recovery and upgrade changes together.

Automatic full recovery remains local midnight. Both gold sources, existing balances, general-store prices, request rewards, guild-rank thresholds and enemy/companion scaling remain. Google setup stays deferred.

The [economy report](economy-1.22/README.md) compares 160 simulated accounts at days 7 and 30. All eight moderate equipment-first classes sustain three/five encounters per day in those fixtures. Optional workshop spending lowers wallets, but surplus gold and high-stat challenge remain open.
