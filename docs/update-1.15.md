# Version 1.15.0 — daily requests, measured work and expedition reserves

## Expedition reserves

HP and MP carry between battles, including Continue, retreat, defeat, map visits and retries. Each member has a separate saved reserve, including benched companions; the player keeps the same reserve after changing class or equipment. Healing skills, Guard's existing MP recovery and consumables still work.

Full recovery occurs at **12:00 AM in the device's local time**. An open app refreshes then; returning after midnight also refreshes. Active battles retain their enemies, rounds, abilities and effects while party HP/MP recover. Lost battles remain lost and may be retried after recovery. A fully defeated party cannot begin another encounter or exploration until midnight or a rested companion joins.

Older saves initialize reserves from their saved battle rather than granting a refill on upgrade. Member records are included in ordinary local saves and character backups. The storage key and package identity are unchanged.

## P03 — faster daily request flow

- Current work appears above the unchanged Guild Card, with the first four unfinished commitments and a link to all Taken requests. Ready submissions appear first.
- Class and career shortcuts open the corresponding daily boards directly. Board shortcuts also switch to the selected class, profession or vocation without opening Filters. Universal requests remain behind their six stat boards.
- The parchment header and action footer stay visible while instructions scroll. Take, Submit, release and the first blocking requirement stay within reach. Checklists appear before the longer instructions. Rank selection uses a single selector with the chosen variant's details.
- Manual acceptance, one selected rank per group, cooldowns, calendar deadlines, original class/career credit and duplicate-submission protections remain in force.

## P04 — optional measured results

Accepted requests can record exercise/variation, sets, reps per set (or total reps without sets), load, duration, distance, completed items and a short outcome. All measurements are optional; submitting without a record still works. Imperial load/distance are converted to canonical kilograms/kilometres, then displayed using the character's unit preference.

Results save with the request's completion. Journey includes the record in its chronology and adds **Practice results** charts for volume, duration, distance, load and completed items. Exercise names/variations keep series distinct. These records are separate from standardized assessment and exam results; higher work volume alone does not establish higher maximum strength or greater aerobic fitness.

Measurements do not change rewards, grant base stats or pass an exam. The chosen request still requires its full prescribed outcome. Results currently attach at submission; independent activity entry, later record editing and wearable imports remain future work.

A daily request's parchment previews matching accepted weekly/monthly goals that would receive a practice day. Each qualifying date counts once per goal, even with several daily sessions. Those goals retain their own outcome, acceptance, deadline, checklist and submission. Earlier qualifying submissions within the calendar period still count after taking a goal. Adding measurements creates no extra completion or payout.

Support-role tuning, later regions and the full economy remain P02/P08 work. Native midnight/resume behavior, keyboards, reminders, sign-in and upgrades require physical Android checks. This release does not establish live Firebase readiness.
