## Evidence limits

Ability samples and career rubrics are self-reviewed, provisional practice rules. Source version and subject names help preserve comparisons but cannot prove that a marking guide is valid or an artifact meets it. Simulated accounts use declared outcomes; software selection, reward and save correctness do not establish human learning, fitness gains or retention. The [research record](proficiency-1.26/RESEARCH.md) distinguishes source access from background references and authored thresholds.

Companion-led support/career play remains valid; party reserves still reset automatically at local midnight, not every encounter. Base-stat gains remain tied to existing milestones/exams and special rewards. Accepted contracts and earned histories remain.
