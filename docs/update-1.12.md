# Version 1.12.0 — parchment commissions and longer routes

Objective details now open as a parchment commission lifted from the board: irregular paper edges, a pinhole, folded texture, the new guild crest and a wax rank seal. Each commission shows the selected rank's activity, reward, instructions, suggested record and completion criteria. Pushup variants include set volume, form and rests; subnet drills include the addresses to calculate and how to check them; study, communication, Scripture, career and conditioning requests have their own protocols. Existing acceptance, eligibility, checklists and reward rules remain in place.

The compact board uses distinct attribute symbols and visible **STR / DEX / CON / INT / WIS / CHA** codes. A smaller category badge still identifies universal, class, profession or vocation work, alongside the existing real-world/adventure indicator. The board stays compact; opening a commission reveals the details.

All **24 places have ten stage battles**, for **240 stages** across eight regions. The area panel shows **one current stage**, its enemies, loot and route progress. Winning and continuing advances directly to the next encounter; completing the route opens repeatable exploration. Area guardians occupy the tenth stage. Roaming encounters continue exploration rather than sending the party back into story stages.

Existing saves retain their cleared encounter IDs, recruited companions, resources and rewards. A one-time migration preserves any area access and repeatable exploration already unlocked under the four-stage routes. Newly added stages remain available to complete; migration does not award their clears or loot. New characters complete the ten-stage route before opening exploration.

The new guild emblem is a crossed sword and crystal staff over a shield. The navigation icon, bundled crest, favicon, Android splash/launcher vector and notification mark use this design. The engraved Guild Card and rank metals are unchanged.

Utility labels in the Guild, desktop request filters, selected areas and adventure tabs now have stronger text contrast.
