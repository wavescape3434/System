# Class register

All nonbasic classes require above-average stats. Advanced paths can qualify at registration; Prestige and Ascendant paths also require prior class progression. Newly entered paths require a trial after registration. Every fifth class level requires a passed improvement exam; unlocked classes and earlier progress remain available.

## Basic

### Warrior

A front-line weapon fighter who breaks enemy formations.

- **Real-world goal:** Build muscular strength with bodyweight exercises or weights.
- **Archetypes:** 100% Fighter
- **Requirements:** Open to every guild member
- **Signature:** Iron Cleave — Strike every enemy with a sweeping physical attack.

### Sentinel

A shield bearer who absorbs attacks and protects the party.

- **Real-world goal:** Build cardiovascular endurance, recovery habits, and consistent movement.
- **Archetypes:** 100% Guardian
- **Requirements:** Open to every guild member
- **Signature:** Sentinel’s Challenge — Guard and draw enemy attacks for two rounds.

### Ranger

A bow fighter and trail scout who finds openings at range.

- **Real-world goal:** Improve mobility, balance, outdoor activity, and navigation.
- **Archetypes:** 100% Ranger
- **Requirements:** Open to every guild member
- **Signature:** Hawk’s Mark — A focused attack that bypasses armor.

### Rogue

A precise skirmisher who exploits exposed defenses.

- **Real-world goal:** Practice coordination, agility, observation, and deliberate planning.
- **Archetypes:** 100% Rogue
- **Requirements:** Open to every guild member
- **Signature:** Opening Gambit — Expose one enemy: reduce its defense by 3.

### Scholar

An apprentice of arcane theory who turns knowledge into spells.

- **Real-world goal:** Study consistently, improve recall, and apply new information.
- **Archetypes:** 100% Mage
- **Requirements:** Open to every guild member
- **Signature:** Aether Bolt — Deal concentrated magic damage to one enemy.

### Disciple

A healer who keeps companions standing through difficult battles.

- **Real-world goal:** Understand Christian Scripture, pray, and put faith into practical service.
- **Archetypes:** 100% Cleric
- **Requirements:** Open to every guild member
- **Signature:** Prayer of Restoration — Restore a living ally’s HP.

### Bard

A performer whose songs strengthen the entire party.

- **Real-world goal:** Build communication, relationships, creativity, and public speaking.
- **Archetypes:** 100% Bard
- **Requirements:** Open to every guild member
- **Signature:** Traveler’s Refrain — Raise the party’s attack and magic for the battle.

### Artificer

A maker who fights with devices, wards, and field repairs.

- **Real-world goal:** Learn technical concepts through building, testing, and documenting projects.
- **Archetypes:** 100% Artificer
- **Requirements:** Open to every guild member
- **Signature:** Field Mending — Restore a living ally’s HP.

## Advanced
